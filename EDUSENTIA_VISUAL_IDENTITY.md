# Edusentia Visual Identity

## Brand architecture

- Company: **Edusentia Technologies**
- Product: **Edusentia**
- Master edition: **Edusentia Enterprise**
- School edition: **Edusentia School**
- Reporting family: **Edusentia Reports**
- Backup and recovery: **Edusentia Vault**
- Licensing and deployment: **Edusentia Control**
- Positioning: **The Academic Operations Platform**

## Mark

The r37 identity uses a geometric **E** mark built from three horizontal academic/data layers and an ascending blue spine. The mark is deliberately simple so it remains legible at favicon, mobile-app and school-attribution sizes.

Approved files:

- `assets/edusentia-logo.png` - primary 512px raster mark
- `assets/edusentia-logo-512.png` - PWA/app icon
- `assets/edusentia-logo-192.png` - compact/PWA icon
- `assets/edusentia-mark.svg` - scalable vector mark

## Colour system

- Edusentia Navy: `#072863`
- Edusentia Blue: `#105ED2`
- Edusentia Green: `#00B552`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Light application background: `#F3F6FA`

## Usage

Edusentia Enterprise uses the Edusentia mark as its primary product identity. Licensed schools keep their own school crest/logo as the primary school identity and use Edusentia as the technology-provider identity. Internal `RCE` names are compatibility identifiers and must not be renamed solely for branding.
